<h1>Release v0.51.0</h1> <p>We are excited to announce the release of Perses v0.51.0!</p> <p>This release brings several new features, improvements, and bug fixes to enhance your experience with Perses.</p> <p>It is a significant step forward in the evolution of Perses, and we are thrilled to share it with you. We have been working hard to bring you a more powerful and flexible platform for your observability needs.</p> <p>This release includes a new plugin architecture, new panel, new variable, a new repository <a href="https://github.com/perses/plugins">perses/plugins</a> containing all default plugins supported by Perses.</p> <p>We have also made significant improvements to the documentation, highlighting features that were implemented some time ago but not well advertised, such as datasource discovery and ephemeral dashboards.</p>
