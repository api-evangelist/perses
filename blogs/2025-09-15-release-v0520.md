<h1>Release v0.52.0</h1> <p>The release of Perses v0.52.0 introduces significant new features and improvements.</p> <p>This update expands Perses' observability capabilities by introducing support for logging and continuous profiling, along with new plugins for Pyroscope, Flame Chart, Loki, and log visualization.</p> <p>Additional enhancements include a heatmap panel for Prometheus histograms, a quick query viewer, panel group variable repeat, usability improvements, and upgraded CLI commands to streamline plugin development and schema testing.</p>
